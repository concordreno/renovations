
# Home Renovation Guide in North York & Toronto

Planning a home renovation in North York, Toronto,
or the GTA? A well-planned renovation can improve
your home's comfort, functionality, and appearance.

## Full Home Renovation Planning

Before starting a renovation, consider:

- Establishing a realistic project budget
- Planning kitchen and bathroom improvements
- Choosing suitable flooring and finishes
- Reviewing structural and permit requirements
- Working with experienced renovation professionals

## Kitchen and Bathroom Renovations

Kitchens and bathrooms require careful planning.
Consider storage, lighting, ventilation, moisture
protection, and durable materials when selecting
fixtures and finishes.
